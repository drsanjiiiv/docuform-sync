# Privacy Policy for DocuForm Sync

Your privacy is a top priority. DocuForm Sync handles data strictly in compliance with Google API User Data Policies.

1. **Data Access & Scope:** The add-on requests access to Google Forms, Google Sheets (read-only), and script triggers solely to execute your configured sync tasks (e.g., reading a sheet column and updating the options of your form questions).
2. **Data Storage:** DocuForm Sync operates entirely within your isolated Google Apps Script environment. We do not transmit, collect, or store your form responses, spreadsheet data, or personal information on external third-party servers. No user data is stored by the add-on itself; all data remains inside your own Google Workspace files.
3. **Third-Party Sharing:** No user data is ever shared, sold, or used for advertising purposes. All data operations remain localized to your active Google Workspace ecosystem. User data is used solely to provide the column-to-question sync features visible in the add-on interface, and is never used to create, train, or improve any AI/ML model.
4. **Limited Use Disclosure:** The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For details on managing or deleting your data, please see the DocuForm Sync support documentation.
